What the checks mean
  • Gouges: the tool cuts into the finished part.
  • Rapid collisions: a rapid (G0) move goes through stock or the part.
  • Over-flute / shank: the tool cuts above its flutes, so the shank would rub.
  • Holder: the holder comes closer than 1.0 mm (0.039 in) to the stock or part.
  • Probe clcheck: the finish stays within 0.005 mm (0.0002 in) of the model at every probe point. 0 misses needed.
  • Engagement (warning, not a failure): the tool takes more than 1.2× the optimal load (2.438 mm, 0.096 in, for the default) or more than 1.5× the stepover. The feed is slowed there.
